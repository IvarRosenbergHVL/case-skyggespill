# Plausibility review 01 — Norwegian grounding

Status: architect research / design constraint. Not player-facing.

## Research conclusions

### Morozov / Ingrid
Norwegian PST material explicitly describes foreign intelligence officers operating under diplomatic cover, contact at seminars/conferences, cultivation in stages, interest in both classified and unclassified sensitive information, and collection concerning internal conflicts, dissatisfaction and vulnerabilities. Political processes and decision-makers are relevant intelligence targets.

Architecture consequence: Ingrid does not need access to state secrets to be valuable. Her political trajectory, internal network knowledge, assessment of personalities and insight into Nystart's internal security-policy tensions are sufficient to make gradual cultivation credible.

Avoid: a melodramatic 'spy recruitment' scene or Morozov asking directly for classified documents. Prefer progressive normalisation of increasingly specific contextual questions.

### Ingrid's realization artifact
Do not use a secret PST case file handed to Ingrid. A more credible source is an unclassified security-awareness briefing, parliamentary/party security seminar handout, public-sector briefing extract, or other legitimate threat-awareness material describing a pattern of cultivation and the kinds of unclassified political information sought.

The decisive realization should arise because Ingrid recognises her own contact pattern against that general pattern and then sees that a distinctive set of details she shared has been operationally useful elsewhere. Exact institution remains fictional/generic until geography and her formal role are locked.

### Thomas financial scheme
Norwegian political parties and party units have accounting/reporting obligations, and donor disclosure thresholds exist. This makes a crude hidden 'large donation' unnecessarily implausible and easy to expose.

Prefer procurement/invoice abuse: Thomas controls communications/event procurement during rapid growth. Inflated or partly fictitious invoices make legitimate-looking campaign expenditure absorb costs while an intermediary supplier controlled through an associate returns value, pays undeclared campaign expenses, or benefits Thomas personally. The party leadership need not know.

The scheme should leave ordinary business evidence: invoice numbers, company records, supplier contacts, repeated production references, address/contact overlap and procurement patterns. No specialist forensic accounting should be required.

### Martin murder method
Retain vehicle/bicycle homicide only if evidence is modest and convergent. Avoid a perfect CSI reconstruction. Police can initially treat a night-time bicycle death as an accident if scene circumstances permit, but the player package must later support a second-vehicle inference through several ordinary observations rather than one miraculous trace.

Recommended physical architecture: low-speed/medium-speed lateral or rear-quarter vehicle contact on a poorly observed road segment, followed by a secondary impact/fall. Thomas does not meticulously stage a laboratory-perfect scene; he leaves because the scene plausibly resembles a solo crash at first glance. This is more credible than moving the body/bicycle extensively.

Avoid: precise speed calculations, microscopic paint matching, impossible CCTV coverage, or a broken component that uniquely identifies a car model.

## Architecture constraints added
- Russia thread uses public/unclassified and personally held evidence only.
- Ingrid is valuable because of influence/network/context, not classified access.
- Thomas scheme is procurement fraud/concealed benefit, not a simplistic undeclared Russian donation.
- Solveig and ordinary Nystart leadership remain unaware.
- Martin can reach Thomas through public company data plus party/supplier material.
- Incident 02 scene is misleading by natural appearance and Thomas's departure, not elaborate post-crash staging.
- Physical homicide conclusion requires at least two independent scene/damage indicators.
- Thomas identity requires separate motive + opportunity + vehicle corroboration.
