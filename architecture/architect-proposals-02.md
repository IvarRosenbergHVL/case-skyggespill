# Architect proposals 02 — critical truth and phase bridge

Status: architect proposal; not player-facing; requires creator review before lock.

## 1. Ingrid's realization: the briefing comes back to her

The strongest architecture is not that Ingrid suddenly receives a spy confession. She recognises **her own information coming back through a channel where it should not exist**.

Earlier, Morozov has repeatedly asked Ingrid for apparently harmless political context: who inside Nystart actually influences security policy, which internal disagreements are real, who might support a softer NATO line, and how particular people would react to a hypothetical proposal. Ingrid has treated this as diplomatic/political conversation, not delivery of classified information.

At a later security/policy meeting Ingrid receives or sees a legitimate briefing/summary describing a Russian approach, talking point or influence assessment. A distinctive combination of details mirrors context she previously gave only to Morozov. No single detail proves espionage; the combination does. Ingrid realises that her conversations were not merely intellectual exchange: she has been profiled and her information operationalised.

This gives us a CER-strong causal chain:

1. Ingrid sees the briefing/summary.
2. She recognises several details from prior Morozov conversations.
3. She revisits earlier messages/calendar notes and understands the progression of his questions.
4. She becomes frightened not because she knowingly passed secrets, but because she now understands the purpose of the relationship.
5. She decides that internal party crisis-management is insufficient.
6. She contacts Martin, a security-policy opponent who had publicly warned about precisely this kind of cultivation.
7. Hovden learns she intends to disclose the relationship and confronts her.

### Evidence consequences

This truth naturally creates both textual and visual evidence:

- earlier messages or calendar entries showing the gradual question pattern;
- a conference photograph containing Morozov before players know who he is;
- the later public photograph that permits visual identity matching;
- Ingrid's annotations on the briefing/summary, potentially circling or underlining the specific constellation she recognises;
- Martin's later reconstruction of the same progression.

The decisive insight must not be 'a document says Morozov was a spy'. Players should reconstruct why Ingrid herself became convinced.

## 2. Why Martin investigates despite Hovden's confession

Ingrid's final private approach to Martin should be concrete enough to create an unresolved obligation but not disclose the solution. Proposed message:

> Jeg tror du hadde rett om noe. Jeg trenger å vise deg hvorfor. Ikke på telefon. Kan vi møtes i morgen?

The exact wording remains subject to document-writing review, but the semantic content is architectural: **you were right / I have something to show you / not by phone / imminent meeting**.

Martin knows what their public disagreement concerned: Russian influence, political naivety and the vulnerability of informal political contacts. When Hovden later explains Ingrid's death entirely as a secret love affair, Martin does not conclude Hovden is innocent. He concludes that the explanation for *why Ingrid was in crisis immediately before her death* is missing.

This preserves phase 1: Hovden is correctly identified as killer. Phase 2 asks a different question: why did he kill her?

## 3. Thomas's secondary secret: narrow the scheme

Avoid a vague 'party corruption' plot. Proposed architecture is a **Thomas-controlled supplier loop**.

Thomas has a legitimate communications/events consultancy or controls procurement relationships around Nystart. During rapid party growth, several small suppliers invoice for campaign/event/communications work. A subset of invoices are inflated or partly fictitious. Money is routed through an intermediary company controlled through a trusted associate and used to cover campaign infrastructure and organisational costs that Nystart could not otherwise afford. Thomas also extracts personal benefit, making the exposure personally criminal rather than merely an arguable campaign-finance technicality.

Important constraints:

- Solveig and ordinary Nystart members do not know the scheme.
- Russia has no financial role in it.
- Martin initially investigates the supplier network because he suspects covert foreign financing.
- The evidence instead converges on Thomas.
- The trail must be understandable visually and textually without accounting expertise: repeated reference formats, shared address/contact detail, recurring visual identity, invoice sequence, supplier relationship and a simple money-flow explanation.
- The amount must be large enough and repeated enough that exposure plausibly threatens prosecution, livelihood and political ruin.

This gives Thomas a stronger motive than 'bad publicity': Martin is about to expose a sustained economic crime with evidence linking Thomas personally to it.

## 4. Murder 2 — architecture recommendation

Keep the vehicle/bicycle concept for now, but do not lock the exact mechanics yet.

Thomas arranges or accepts a late meeting with Martin after learning Martin has connected him to the supplier loop. Martin travels by bicycle. The encounter ends with Thomas deliberately using the vehicle against Martin and then leaving the scene arranged to resemble a serious single-bicycle crash.

The case must prove homicide through **convergence**, not CSI magic:

- scene geometry inconsistent with a simple loss of control;
- bicycle/contact damage inconsistent with the apparent fixed object/road feature;
- a small transferable/physical trace consistent with vehicle contact;
- route/timing placing a vehicle in the relevant window;
- later evidence connecting Thomas's vehicle to recent unexplained damage/repair;
- Martin's final investigative activity establishing motive.

No single scratch or photograph identifies Thomas. Physical evidence establishes another vehicle; documentary/timeline evidence establishes opportunity; financial evidence establishes motive; cross-phase provenance establishes Thomas.

## 5. Phase-1 latent Thomas artifact

Preferred design: Thomas supplies players with a **press/event archive package** while genuinely helping reconstruct Ingrid's final days. One item contains a mundane event photograph, sponsor/supplier credit, production label, filename convention, or small visual mark from the same supplier ecosystem later found in Martin's financing material.

The clue must be naturally present because Thomas really did organise or archive the event. It is not a planted clue and is not suspicious in phase 1. In phase 2, Martin's material gives players a reason to recognise the same supplier identity/reference and physically retrieve the old item.

This is stronger than hiding an incriminating invoice in phase 1: Thomas can plausibly provide it without behaving irrationally, and the visual/provenance relationship only becomes incriminating after Martin discovers the network.

## 6. Balance rule

Skyggespill is not to become a photo-comparison puzzle. The architecture should alternate investigative modes:

- textual claim and testimony;
- timeline and chronology;
- visual observation;
- document provenance;
- relationship inference;
- physical/scene evidence;
- rereading and recontextualisation.

Visual evidence should reveal relationships or contradictions that are difficult to express as prose without giving the deduction away. Text should remain the main carrier of character, motive, testimony, politics, context and narrative consequence.
